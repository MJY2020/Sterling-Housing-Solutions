Damp & mould inspections

A focused inspection service designed to document the visible condition, identify likely contributing factors and provide practical next steps.

Visual condition surveyInspection of affected rooms, surfaces and visible signs of damp, mould and condensation.
Moisture investigationRelevant moisture readings and observations to help distinguish likely causes.
Ventilation & condensation reviewReview of ventilation, condensation patterns and obvious contributing property conditions.
Photographic reportA structured record of findings with photographs, observations and recommended next actions.
Simple process

STEP 02

Inspection arranged

The property is booked for an independent damp and mould inspection.

STEP 03

Report issued

Findings, photographs and recommended actions are returned to the client.

STEP 04

Outcome checked

An optional follow-up visit can document whether the issue has reappeared after works.
