Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Sterling Housing Solutions uses information submitted through this website to respond to damp and mould inspection enquiries and manage follow-up communication.

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, email address, telephone number, property address, landlord, agent, council or housing provider details, and information you choose to provide about the reported damp or mould issue.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to your enquiry.
  • To arrange or discuss damp and mould inspection services.
  • To keep an internal record of enquiry status and notes.
  • To send enquiry notification emails to Sterling Housing Solutions.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with relevant staff, service providers used to operate the website or email delivery, and where required by law.

Storage

Website enquiries are stored on the website server or the selected production database/storage provider. Access is restricted to the admin area.

Photos

Photo uploads are currently disabled on the website. If photos are needed, Sterling Housing Solutions may request them separately using an appropriate secure method.

Your Rights

You can ask to access, correct, or delete personal information held about you, subject to any legal or business record requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Sterling Housing Solutions using the enquiry email address shown or configured for this website.
