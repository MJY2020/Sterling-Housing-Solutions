Website Use

This website provides information about Sterling Housing Solutions and allows users to submit damp and mould inspection enquiries.

No Emergency Service

The enquiry form is not an emergency reporting service. If there is an urgent safety issue, contact your landlord, housing provider, local authority, emergency contractor, or emergency services as appropriate.

Information Submitted

You are responsible for ensuring the information you submit is accurate and that you have permission to provide details about a property or housing issue.

Inspection Services

Submitting an enquiry does not create a contract. Any inspection, report, price, appointment or scope of work must be confirmed directly by Sterling Housing Solutions.

Website Availability

We aim to keep the website available, but access may be interrupted for maintenance, hosting issues, or technical problems.

Privacy

Personal information submitted through the website is handled under the Privacy Policy.
